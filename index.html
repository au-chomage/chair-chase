About

The idea

Somewhere in an open-plan office, four colleagues have decided the fastest way between two desks is a racing line. Chair Chase is five laps of that argument, across five workplaces, on a chair that was never designed for this.

Support the game

Chair Chase is free, carries no ads, and is made by one small studio. If it has cost you an afternoon you were supposed to spend working, you can buy us a coffee.

Controls

  • ▲Accelerate
  • ▼Brake and reverse
  • SpaceBoost, once the bar is full
  • VHold through a corner to drift
  • XYour character’s special ability
  • FSwitch to the first-person view
  • EscPause

On a phone, steer with the stick and use the buttons on the right. Turn the handset sideways to race.

Drifting is worth learning

Hold V through a corner and the chair steps out sideways. The longer you hold a clean slide, the bigger the payout when you let go: a burst of speed, and a large chunk of your boost bar refilled. Boost otherwise takes eight seconds to recharge on its own, so drifting well is the difference between three boosts a lap and five.

Every chair slides differently. The Gaming Chair swings out furthest and is hardest to hold; the Folding Chair barely steps out at all but costs almost nothing to slide.

The leaderboard

Finish a race and your best lap, the ground you covered and the number of HR complaints you generated all go to a shared board. Whoever runs the most races in a day takes Employee of the Month, and their face goes on the monitor in the chair select screen until somebody beats them.
